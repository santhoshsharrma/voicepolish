Questions

Is my audio stored?

Not by us. Your recording is sent once to a transcription service to get the text, and is not saved.

Does polishing change my meaning?

It’s instructed not to add facts or change intent. Always review the result; you can edit it or compare with the original.

Why is the mic blocked?

Allow microphone access in your browser’s site settings. If the page is embedded, open it in its own tab.
